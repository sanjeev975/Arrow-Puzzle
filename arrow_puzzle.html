💡 How to Play

🚀
1. Tap to Launch
Every arrow points in a cardinal direction (Up, Right, Down, Left). Tapping an arrow shoots it forward in its pointing direction.
✨
2. Clear Paths Escape
If there are no other arrows or obstacles between the arrow and the edge of the board, it flies off the screen and clears!
💥
3. Avoid Collisions ("Bonks")
If an arrow's flight path is blocked by another arrow, it recoils back to its place with a "bonk" sound, and flashes the blocking arrow in red. You must clear the blocker first!
⭐
4. Special Arrows & Combos

↻ Rotator Arrow: If blocked, tapping it rotates it 90° clockwise to explore new escape routes!

↔ / ↕ Dual Arrow: Has two opposite arrowheads and will exit in whichever direction is clear!

🔥 Combo Multiplier: Rapidly clearing arrows in succession raises your combo multiplier and plays ascending musical scale chimes.
